# Trip to the coast

A long weekend in May, three of us, one car.

## Before we go

- [x] Book the cabin (two nights)
- [x] Check the tyres and the spare
- [ ] Download the offline maps
- [ ] Pack the camera and the tripod

## The route

| Day | From | To | Hours |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Friday | Home | Lighthouse Point | 4 |
| Saturday | Lighthouse Point | Seal Bay | 1.5 |
| Sunday | Seal Bay | Home | 5 |

> Leave before seven on Friday: the bridge is slow after nine.

## Food

1. Breakfast at the cabin
2. Fish and chips at the harbour on Saturday
3. Whatever the bakery has left on Sunday

The cabin's check-in code comes by message the day before. Bring `cash` for the parking at Seal Bay.
