# Brand icons

A small selection (about 95) of the logos from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons)
(`icons/`, with each brand's colour in `colors.json`), used by `liki-build` to build an icon pack
of app logos. Simple Icons' logo files are released under CC0 1.0; the logos themselves remain trademarks
of their owners and are used here only to stand for the apps they belong to.

`native/` holds Labyrinth's own glyphs (Minos, Settings, Calculator, the trash) in the same single-colour form, so
they match when a pack is built.

Agora (the Marketplace) also uses this set for apps that ship no icon of their own (terminal programs such as lazygit, Docker and
Podman): the logo named by the catalog's `logo` field, white on a tile of the brand's colour.
